THE LEGEND

Five kinds of doors, one blueprint.

"Endpoint protection" isn't one product — it's coverage across every device type that touches your network. Most breaches start at the one nobody mapped.

Servers

Highest value, usually best protected. The blind spots are patch timing and lateral movement once one is compromised.

Laptops & workstations

Leave the network daily. The single most common entry point for ransomware in businesses under 500 employees.

Mobile & remote

Personal phones checking company email. Rarely covered by traditional antivirus, almost never by MDM.

POS & IoT

Cameras, card readers, smart locks. Long-lived firmware that nobody owns updating.
